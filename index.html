Questions providers ask

How do students find these pages?

Each page is built around what students type, like "ServSafe classes [your city]." It's made by the team whose Food Safety Made Easy city pages already rank on page one for those searches.

Who gets the three spots?

Local instructors and proctors who apply, until a city's three are filled. After that, new providers join the waitlist. Students see every provider on the page, ordered by how well each fits their answers.

Where do my details come from?

Your free Food Safety Made Easy listing. Applying sets it up if you don't have one, and any change you make there shows on your city page.

What do I pay during the free months?

Nothing. Before your third month ends, choose monthly or yearly to keep your spot, or let it go.

What if my city doesn't have a page yet?

Apply anyway. We open new cities as providers sign up for them.

Do you take a cut of my classes?

No. Students book and pay you directly. Your plan is the only cost.
